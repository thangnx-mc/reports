## Executive Summary

A live-test investigation was conducted following reported failures surrounding the detection and handling of the **green Play button / room-creation interface**. 

Analysis of `dist\DungeonQuestMacro\macro_audit.log`, diagnostic screen captures (`diagnostic_final_frame.png` and `diagnostic_raw_frame.png`), fixture datasets, and state-machine execution traces revealed two distinct, interacting failure mechanisms:

1. **Title Screen Stall & State-Variable Leak Across Live Test Restarts**:
   - In **Session 1 (23:40:44 – 23:40:57)**, the macro started on the Roblox title screen, correctly identified `GameState.TITLE_SPLASH`, and clicked the central yellow `TITLE_PLAY_BUTTON` at calibrated `(487, 449)` (screen `(913, 853)` on HWND 132986). However, the client did not dismiss the title screen. In `WAIT_FOR_LOBBY`, the retry handler failed to re-acquire the button candidate because the yellow button contour touched the razor-edge boundary ($y=399$) of the ROI. Because `find_button` returned `None`, the retry timer did not reset, causing `title_play_retries` to increment on successive frames without clicking and halting into `HALTED_SAFETY` after 13 seconds.
   - In **Session 2 (23:41:27 – 23:41:55)**, when the user restarted the live test via hotkey `;`, `gui_app.py` failed to reset `title_play_clicked` and `title_play_retries`. The leaked `title_play_clicked=True` and `title_play_retries=2` completely blocked re-dispatching clicks in `TITLE_SCREEN`, causing the macro to idle for 25 seconds with 0 clicks dispatched until the user pressed the stop hotkey `'`.

2. **Detector Ambiguity Between Green Lobby Play and Title Screen Updates Button**:
   - `OpenCVVisionProvider.find_ui_element(frame, "play_green_lobby")` inspects ROI `hsv[480:540, 860:1010]` for green contours ($bw > 80, bh > 25$).
   - On the Title Screen (`diagnostic_final_frame.png`), the green **"Updates"** button sits at $x \in [877, 1002], y \in [480, 521]$ ($bw=125, bh=41$). As a result, querying `play_green_lobby` on the Title Screen produces an immediate false-positive match (`bounding_box=(877, 480, 125, 41), center=(939, 500), conf=0.95`).
   - Furthermore, on the actual room-creation menu (`DUNGEON_CREATE_MENU` / `media_1791518341534.png`), the green lobby Play button is still visible in the background town square at `(892, 491, 111, 36)`. However, the button that creates a room on that screen is **RED** (`CREATE_LOBBY_RED` at center `(512, 376)`). There is no green button belonging to the room-creation modal itself.

## 2. Reconstruct the Failure

### 2.1 Which state was active when the room-creation interface appeared?
- `[FACT]`: In both Session 1 and Session 2, the macro never reached the room-creation interface (`DUNGEON_CREATE_MENU`) or the lobby (`IN_LOBBY`). The macro remained held in `TITLE_SCREEN` / `WAIT_FOR_LOBBY`.
- `[OBSERVATION]`: If the user was referring to the main lobby interface where the green `PLAY` button is clicked to enter room-creation, the macro never reached `IN_LOBBY` because the title screen was not dismissed.
- `[FACT]`: On the actual room-creation screens (`LOBBY_MODE_SELECTION` and `DUNGEON_CREATE_MENU`), the macro state machine enters `MacroState.SELECTING_DUNGEON` (`src/state_machine.py` line 775).

### 2.2 Did the macro correctly identify that it was on the room-creation screen?
- `[FACT]`: During the live test sessions, the vision classifier returned `GameState.TITLE_SPLASH` with confidence `0.98` across all frames. It did not classify the screen as `DUNGEON_CREATE_MENU`.
- `[OBSERVATION]`: However, when `OpenCVVisionProvider.find_ui_element(img, "play_green_lobby")` is evaluated on the title screen frame (`diagnostic_final_frame.png`), it returns a positive match (`conf=0.95`) on the green "Updates" button.

### 2.3 Did the vision detector inspect the correct screen region?
- `[FACT]`: 
  - For `play_green_lobby` (the green lobby Play button): the detector inspects `hsv[480:540, 860:1010]`. In the real lobby, the green `PLAY` button is located at `[496:532, 891:1003]` (matches ROI). On the title screen, the green "Updates" button is located at `[480:521, 877:1002]` (also matches ROI).
  - For `title_play_button` (yellow title Play button): the detector inspects `y_start = int(h * 0.74) = 399` to `y_end = int(h * 0.94) = 507`, and `x_start = int(w * 0.40) = 409` to `x_end = int(w * 0.60) = 614`. On `diagnostic_final_frame.png` ($540 \times 1024$), the yellow Play button's top edge is at $y=399$ exactly.

### 2.4 Did it detect any candidate Play button?
- `[FACT]`:
  - **On Title Screen (`diagnostic_final_frame.png`)**:
    - `title_play_button`: Candidate contour detected at `bounding_box=(463, 399, 131, 81)`, center `(528, 439)`, area `4124.5`, aspect ratio `1.62`, confidence `0.96`.
    - `play_green_lobby`: Candidate contour detected at `bounding_box=(877, 480, 125, 41)`, center `(939, 500)`, area `4688`, confidence `0.95`. This was the "Updates" button.
  - **On Room Creation Menu (`DUNGEON_CREATE_MENU` / `media_1791518341534.png`)**:
    - `play_green_lobby`: Candidate contour detected at `bounding_box=(892, 491, 111, 36)`, center `(947, 509)`, confidence `0.95` (background town square button).
    - `create_lobby_red`: Candidate contour detected at `bounding_box=(466, 345, 92, 63)`, center `(512, 376)`, confidence `0.95`.
    - There is no green button belonging to the room-creation modal.

### 2.5 Did it confuse the green room-creation Play button with the yellow title-screen Play button or the green Gate #1 Start button?
- `[FACT]`: 
  1. The vision engine's `find_ui_element(frame, "play_green_lobby")` cannot distinguish between the lobby's green `PLAY` button and the title screen's green `Updates` button because both share the exact same ROI `[480:540, 860:1010]` and HSV green thresholds `[35, 80, 80]` to `[85, 255, 255]`.
  2. In `detect_state()`, `LOBBY_IDLE` is classified if `lb_green >= 1500` in `[480:540, 860:1010]`. The title screen's Updates button yields 4,688 green pixels in that ROI. `LOBBY_IDLE` is avoided only because `TITLE_SPLASH` is positioned earlier in the `if/elif` order.
  3. The in-dungeon Gate #1 Start button (`START_GREEN_IN_MAP`) is at `[460:530, 680:850]`, which is spatially adjacent to the lobby play button and uses the same HSV green filter.

### 2.6 Was the button absent from the captured frame, obscured, partially clipped, or affected by a stale/incorrect screenshot?
- `[FACT]`: In `diagnostic_final_frame.png`, the yellow title Play button was partially clipped at the top boundary $y=399$ because `y_start = int(h * 0.74)` equals $399$. Contour 7 ($48 \times 11$, aspect 4.36) was sliced off the top of the button, leaving Contour 8 ($131 \times 81$) right on the perimeter.
- `[INFERENCE]`: When the client viewport is $1920 \times 991$ (aspect $1.937$), resizing to $1024 \times 540$ compresses the vertical axis, pushing the button's upper border against $y=399$. Any sub-pixel variation causes `find_button("title_play_button")` to fail.
- `[FACT]`: On the room-creation screen (`DUNGEON_CREATE_MENU`), there is no green Play button inside the modal dialog; the only button that creates the room is the RED `CREATE_LOBBY_RED` button.

### 2.7 Did a timeout, watchdog, state transition, or another detector prevent the Play-button detector from running?
- `[FACT]`: In Session 1, `find_button("title_play_button")` ran and returned `(487, 449)` on tick 1, triggering a click and transition to `WAIT_FOR_LOBBY`. During `WAIT_FOR_LOBBY`, `find_button` returned `None` during retry checks, causing rapid increment of `title_play_retries` without clicking, tripping the 3-attempt timeout at 23:40:57.
- `[FACT]`: In Session 2, `title_play_clicked=True` and `title_play_retries=2` were leaked from Session 1 because `src/gui_app.py` lines 1136–1164 did not reset them. This prevented the state machine from executing the click logic in `TITLE_SCREEN`, causing a 25-second stall until manual stop.

### 2.8 What exact state and error/reason caused the macro to stall, retry, or halt?
- **Session 1**:
  - State: `WAIT_FOR_LOBBY` -> `HALTED_SAFETY`
  - Error: `SAFE HALT ENGAGED: Timeout: Title screen remained visible after 3 attempts clicking Play. Halting input and requiring review.`
- **Session 2**:
  - State: `TITLE_SCREEN` (Stalled / Idle)
  - Cause: Leaked `title_play_clicked=True` and `title_play_retries=2` in `state_machine` state variables across GUI restart, combined with `find_button` returning `None`.

## 5. Minimal Proposed Fix & Regression Test Plan

### 5.1 Minimal Code Fixes (For Subsequent Turn)

1. **Fix `src/gui_app.py` Live Test Reset**:
   In `start_live_test()` (line 1139), explicitly call `self.app.state_machine._reset_run_state()` to clear all run variables including `title_play_clicked`, `title_play_retries`, and `title_play_click_time`.
2. **Fix `src/state_machine.py` Retry Guard**:
   In `WAIT_FOR_LOBBY` (lines 666–678), only increment `self.title_play_retries` and update `self.title_play_click_time` when `btn` is found and a click is actually dispatched. If `btn` is `None`, dispatch click to calibrated fallback coordinates `(512, 440)`.
3. **Fix `src/vision_engine.py` Yellow Play ROI**:
   Change `y_start = int(h * 0.70)` (was `0.74`, expanding from $y=399$ down to $y=378$) and `y_end = int(h * 0.95)` to ensure yellow button contours are never clipped on non-standard client viewports.
4. **Fix `src/vision_engine.py` Disambiguation for `play_green_lobby`**:
   Ensure `play_green_lobby` is only detected when the screen is in `GameState.LOBBY` / `GameState.LOBBY_IDLE`, or reject matches if `title_yellow_px >= 500` or `logo_yellow_px >= 2000` is detected.
5. **Clarify Terminology**:
   Document in the GUI status and logs that the button creating a room inside `DUNGEON_CREATE_MENU` is the **Red** `CREATE_LOBBY_RED` button, while the **Green** `PLAY_GREEN_LOBBY` button is the lobby town square entry point.

### 5.2 Regression Test Plan

- `tests/test_title_screen_lifecycle.py`: Verify that starting from `TITLE_SCREEN`, failing a click, retrying, and restarting via GUI properly resets `title_play_clicked` and `title_play_retries`.
- `tests/test_vision_roi_bounds.py`: Verify that `title_play_button` is reliably detected across varying aspect ratios ($1920 \times 991$, $1920 \times 1080$, $1024 \times 768$) without contour clipping.
- `tests/test_button_disambiguation.py`: Verify that `play_green_lobby` returns `None` on `media_1791518293115.png` (Title Splash) and only matches on `media_1791518308958.jpg` (Lobby).
